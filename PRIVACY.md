# Privacy Policy — Scrimba Section Progress

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Scrimba Section Progress is an independent browser extension maintained by Dagim Shimelis. It is not affiliated with Scrimba.

## Information processed

The extension runs on `https://scrimba.com/*`. On lesson pages it reads the current lesson URL path, section name, completion percentage, and displayed section duration from the page. It uses this information only to display section progress and estimated watched time beside the lesson timer.

## Storage and sharing

This information is processed locally in your browser. Section information and outline-attempt state are cached temporarily in the page's memory and are discarded when the page reloads or closes. The extension does not write this information to cookies, local storage, browser extension storage, or a database.

The extension does not send data to the developer or any third party, sell data, include analytics or advertising, or load remote code. It does not access passwords, authentication cookies, or payment information. No separate extension account is required.

The extension may briefly open the lesson outline to read section data. Scrimba itself may make requests while rendering its own interface; Scrimba's website and its data handling are governed by its own privacy policy.

## Your choices

You can disable or uninstall the extension through Chrome's extensions settings. Reload or close existing Scrimba tabs to remove the injected interface and clear its in-memory data. There is no developer-held user data to delete.

## Changes and contact

Any changes to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date. For questions, contact the maintainer through [the project's GitHub issues](https://github.com/dagim-shimelis/Scrimba-section-progress-extention/issues). Do not include private lesson or account information in a public issue.
